About the site

Cuernavaca is made of layers of power, faith and rest. Between volcanoes and ravines, about fifteen hundred meters up, there is a fortress of Cortés turned into a museum, a sixteenth-century Franciscan cathedral and murals by Diego Rivera. This audio guide takes you through twenty-three stops, from an introduction to the city to the Borda Garden, with routes from one hour up to four hours and forty minutes.
