About the site

Xochicalco was a fortified city that grew on hills around the year six hundred fifty, when the great centers of the previous period fell. This audio guide takes you through its plazas, courts and temples, with the Pyramid of the Feathered Serpents and its calendar in stone, up to the day the city was burned and abandoned.
