About the site

The Metropolitan Cathedral and the Templo Mayor stand just a few metres apart, and together they tell how one city was built on top of another. Here stood the sacred precinct of Tenochtitlan, and on top of it was built the largest cathedral in Latin America, with stones from the Mexica temples. They are part of the Historic Centre, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
