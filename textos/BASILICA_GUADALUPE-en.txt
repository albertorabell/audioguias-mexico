About the site

The Villa de Guadalupe lies at the foot of Tepeyac Hill, in the north of Mexico City, and it is one of the most important places of pilgrimage in the country. Here come together a history that reaches back before the Conquest, a devotion that has lasted for centuries, and several basilicas built in very different eras.
